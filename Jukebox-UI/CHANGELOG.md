# Changelog

## [1.1.0]
- Right-click a jukebox (empty-handed) to open a menu of all music discs.
- Click a disc to play it — no need to have the disc in your inventory.
- Empty-hand right-click on a playing jukebox stops it.
- Virtually-inserted discs don't drop when the jukebox is broken.

## [1.0.0]
- Initial release.
